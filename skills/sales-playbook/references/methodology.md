# Sales Methodology

Default framework is MEDDPICC for qualification and forecasting, Gap Selling for discovery, Challenger for messaging. Swap in Omni's official methodology once known (TODO).

## MEDDPICC

| Letter | Element | What "good" looks like | Evidence required |
|---|---|---|---|
| M | Metrics | Quantified business impact the buyer agrees with, in their numbers | Buyer said or wrote the number |
| E | Economic buyer | Named person who can sign and fund; we've met them | Direct conversation, not secondhand |
| D | Decision criteria | Written or explicitly stated technical and business criteria, ideally shaped by us | Criteria list in buyer's words |
| D | Decision process | Steps, people, and dates from evaluation to signature | Mutual action plan or clear verbal sequence |
| P | Paper process | Legal, security, procurement steps, owners, and timing | Security review / MSA / PO process identified |
| I | Identify pain | Business pain with consequence, tied to a person who feels it | Pain stated by buyer, with impact |
| C | Champion | Has power and influence, sells for us when we're absent, tested | Champion took an action that cost them something (intro to EB, shared internal info) |
| C | Competition | Known alternatives including status quo and build-it-yourself | Named by buyer |

Scoring per element: 0 = unknown, 1 = assumed/secondhand, 2 = partially confirmed, 3 = confirmed by buyer with evidence. A deal with Champion or Economic buyer below 2 should not be in Commit.

## Gap Selling discovery flow

1. **Current state** — what's happening now, technically and in the business. Facts, not feelings.
2. **Problems** — what's wrong with the current state; root cause, not symptoms.
3. **Impact** — what the problems cost (time, money, risk, missed decisions). Get a number.
4. **Future state** — what they want instead, in their words.
5. **The gap** — the distance between current and future. The size of the gap is the size of the deal.
6. **Urgency** — why now; what happens if nothing changes.

## Challenger posture

- **Teach:** bring a commercial insight that reframes how they think about their problem.
- **Tailor:** adapt to what each stakeholder values.
- **Take control:** be comfortable pushing on next steps, process, and money.

## Stage hygiene (placeholder until Omni's stages are known)

| Stage | Exit criteria |
|---|---|
| 1. Discovery | Pain + impact confirmed, champion candidate identified, next meeting booked |
| 2. Qualification | MEDDPICC ≥ 12/24, EB identified, decision process mapped |
| 3. Technical eval / POC | Success criteria written and agreed, eval plan with dates |
| 4. Business case / proposal | Metrics validated by EB, pricing presented, paper process started |
| 5. Negotiation / legal | Redlines in motion, signature date agreed |
| 6. Closed won / lost | Signed, or loss reason documented |
