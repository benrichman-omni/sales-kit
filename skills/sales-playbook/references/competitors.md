# Competitive Landscape

Working notes from general market knowledge, not official Omni battlecards. Replace with internal battlecards after onboarding. Never put unverified competitor claims in writing to a buyer; in emails, compete on the buyer's problem, not by trashing a vendor.

| Competitor | Where you'll meet it | Typical buyer pain with it | Angle to explore (verify) | Landmines |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Looker (Google) | Incumbent at data-mature companies, LookML shops | Slow modeling cycles, business users blocked on data team, cost at renewal | Governed modeling without forcing every question through the modeling bottleneck; familiar concepts for LookML teams | Don't oversell migration ease — TODO: confirm migration tooling |
| Tableau (Salesforce) | Enterprise incumbent, analyst-heavy orgs | Extract sprawl, inconsistent metrics, license cost | Single semantic layer across every user type | Strong viz depth and community; don't fight on chart polish |
| Power BI (Microsoft) | Microsoft-standard enterprises | DAX complexity, bundled "free" perception | TODO | Pricing perception — compete on total cost and data-team time |
| Sigma | Snowflake-centric, spreadsheet-native users | TODO | Omni's spreadsheet + semantic model + AI combination | Sigma also leads with spreadsheets |
| ThoughtSpot | Search/AI-first evaluations | Answer trust, modeling overhead | AI grounded in the semantic model, runs with user permissions | TODO |
| Hex | Data science / notebook-heavy teams | Notebooks don't serve business users at scale | TODO | Often complementary, not competitive |
| Lightdash | dbt-native, smaller teams | TODO | TODO | Open-source price anchor |
| Sisense | Embedded analytics deals | TODO | Embedded styling + semantic layer | Ben has insider context from Sisense days — use judgment and respect any confidentiality obligations |
| Status quo (spreadsheets + SQL + old BI) | Everywhere | Metric drift, analyst backlog, "which number is right?" | Quantify the cost of the backlog and the drift (Gap Selling) | The real competitor in most deals |

## Discovery questions that surface competitive pain

- "When two teams bring different numbers to the same meeting, how does that get resolved today?"
- "What's the typical wait between a business user asking a new question and getting a trusted answer?"
- "What happens at your next BI renewal — is that a decision point or a formality?"
- "Where are people asking AI tools questions about your data today, and do you trust those answers?"
