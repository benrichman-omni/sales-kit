# Ramp Plan

## 30/60/90 framework (adapt to Omni's actual onboarding once known)

**Days 1–30 — Learn**
- Product: get hands-on daily; build a personal demo environment and a 10-minute demo story for one persona.
- Messaging: learn the official pitch, value pillars, and top 3 customer stories cold; pass any certification.
- Market: absorb battlecards for the top 3 competitors and the status quo.
- People: 1:1s with manager, SEs, top AEs, SDR/BDR partners, marketing, CS, and a product manager. Ask each: "What do great AEs here do that others don't?" and "What do you wish new AEs knew?"
- Systems: CRM, sequencing tool, call recording, enablement library. Connect what's possible to Claude.
- Territory: get the account list; tier it (use `account-research` batch mode).
- Fill every `TODO` in `company-context.md` and `competitors.md` you can.

**Days 31–60 — Build**
- Running prospecting cadence on Tier 1 accounts (weekly targets via `weekly-plan`).
- First discovery calls and demos solo; record and self-review against `methodology.md`.
- Shadow live deals in later stages to learn the paper process and deal desk.
- Pipeline target: TODO (set with manager).

**Days 61–90 — Produce**
- Owning a qualified pipeline with MEDDPICC discipline in the tracker.
- First deals progressing to business case.
- Refine personal playbook: which angles, personas, and stories actually convert.

## Learning capture (most important ongoing job)

Whenever Ben shares something learned in onboarding — a product capability, a positioning line, a customer story, a competitor detail, a process rule, a stage definition, quota/territory facts:

1. Restate it in one line.
2. Identify the right home: `company-context.md` (product, ICP, personas, pillars, proof, process), `competitors.md`, `methodology.md` (stages, exit criteria, forecast rules), or `voice.md` (style preferences).
3. Show the exact edit and apply it to the plugin's reference files when the plugin source is editable in this session. If it isn't (an installed, read-only copy), collect the edits into a `playbook-updates.md` file Ben can apply and repackage, and say so.

The goal: by day 30, the playbook reflects Omni's real messaging and every skill in this plugin gets sharper automatically.

## Tracking ramp tasks

Keep a `ramp-checklist.md` in the working folder with the items above as checkboxes plus anything Ben adds; check items off as he reports them done. Surface open items in `weekly-plan`.
